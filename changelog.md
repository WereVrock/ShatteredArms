### Shattered Arms — Changelog

# Horde Edition: 0.5.3
-Heavy graphic optimization
-Some ui quality of life updates
-Scenario player customization
-Ordering multiple units at once preserves relationship
-AI tweaks:
	*Fixed some mistakes
	*Horses now escort archers
-Mercenary Captains added as a way to play the old way without getting under powered due to lack of a commander.
-Goblin commander in Campaign (still a test feature)
-Goblins no longer return back to fight so fast.
-Units allow each other to pass through. 
-Tweaked morale and power effects. Morale no longer drain
-Low discipline troops now look and act like rabble
-Soldiers now fight while moving. Making cavalry be able to pass through enemy unit, moving them down on the way.

# Sun Tzu  Edition : 0.5.2
-Added Campaign difficulty explanations.
-Weakened AI is now really weak
-Best AI has stopped making [some] mistakes
-Now the player can set up weakened vs best AI custom matches
-Unit flags


# Sword Edition: 0.5.1
Same PC Multiplayer, Stamina, bug fixes and balances, campaign
-Nerfed skeletons with a 10% move speed malus.
-Fixed a bug that caused goblin archers to deal the same as humans.
-Skeletons now cost less; skeleton horses cost even less.
-Synced power cost with power displays and AI power read.
-AI bug fixes: archers properly kite, horses properly return if they fail.
-Fixed AI soldier stuttering bug.
-Stamina actually matters now, and walking lowers it. (reduces attack speed and move speed)
-Added more heuristics to AI.
-Added same PC multiplayer.
-Campaign now has 3 difficulty options.
-Goblin power calculations now consider stamina drain.
